# What is CardioAI?

CardioAI is a daily heart-health monitoring tool. You answer a short set of
questions twice a day about how you feel, and CardioAI compares your answers
against your baseline to tell you whether your condition looks good, fair, or
bad — and why.

It is a monitoring aid, not a diagnostic tool. It does not replace a doctor.

# How do I sign up?

Create a patient account with your email address. You will then be asked to
complete an intake form covering your medical history and recent test results.
That form runs once and produces your baseline risk level.

Doctors do not sign up openly. A CardioAI administrator issues an activation
code, which the doctor exchanges for an account.

# How much does it cost?

CardioAI is a student project and is not currently offered as a paid service.

# Who can see my medical record?

Only you, unless you explicitly share it. Booking an appointment with a doctor
does not give that doctor access to your history — sharing is a separate
choice you make from the doctors page, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Every time a doctor opens a record, that access is logged.

# What happens to my daily check-ins?

They are stored against your account so you can see how your condition changes
over time. If you have shared your record with a doctor, they can see your
recent check-ins and the reasons behind each result.

# Can CardioAI tell me if I have heart disease?

No. Only a doctor can diagnose a heart condition, using an examination and
tests such as an ECG or echocardiogram. CardioAI helps you track symptoms and
share them with a doctor.
